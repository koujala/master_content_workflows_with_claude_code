# 🛕 Madurai: The Ancient Temple City

![Madurai Ancient Temple City](madurai_ancient_temple_city.png)

### 🌺 A living city that has never stopped breathing

## 📖 Main Content

Madurai, on the Vaigai River in Tamil Nadu, is widely held to be among the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities, with 2,500+ years of history. It served as the Pandya capital and grew around the Meenakshi Amman Temple, with streets in concentric squares like a lotus. Rebuilt after the 14th century and expanded under Thirumalai Nayak in the 17th, the temple still shapes city life. Today Madurai is a trade, textile and services hub.

## 🔑 Key Concepts

- 👑 **Pandya capital**: seat of a major Tamil kingdom
- 🪷 **Lotus layout**: streets ring the temple
- 🌼 **Madurai Malli**: GI-tagged jasmine, exported worldwide
- 🏙️ **Smart City**: selected under the 2017 mission

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- 🏛️ Heritage and daily life share the same streets
- 🧵 Textiles, trade and agriculture drive the economy
- ⚠️ Exact age claims vary; treat "oldest" with care

Madurai is a 2,500-year-old temple city that still runs on tradition while modernizing.
