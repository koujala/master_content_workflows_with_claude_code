Madurai, in Tamil Nadu, is among India's oldest continuously inhabited cities, with more than 2,500 years of history. Capital of the Pandya kingdom, it grew around the Meenakshi Amman Temple, laid out as concentric streets like a lotus. Rebuilt after the 14th century and expanded in the 17th under Thirumalai Nayak, the temple still anchors daily life. Today the city blends heritage with trade, textiles, GI-tagged Madurai Malli jasmine, and a Smart Cities Mission push.
